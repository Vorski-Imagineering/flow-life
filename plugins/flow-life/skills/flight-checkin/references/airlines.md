# Airline check-in notes

One section per airline: where the online check-in starts, the steps in order, and the
traps. Facts about the site only: never a booking, a name, a number or a date from a
trip. Date each section with the month it was last walked.

Generic advice for an airline not listed here is in `../SKILL.md` §3.

## Eurowings

Last walked: 2026-11.

- **Start**: `https://www.eurowings.com`, then **Check-in** in the top navigation. The
  deep link `/en/manage/check-in.html` returns 404; do not use it.
- **Cookies**: decline the non-essential ones before anything else.
- **Find the booking**: the Eurowings booking code (the carrier PNR, not an OTA's
  number) and the surname.
- **Steps, in order**: Flights → Contact → Seats → Services → Confirm.
  - *Flights*: select the passenger and the flight.
  - *Contact*: email and **mobile number, which is required**; the country code is a
    dropdown beside the number.
  - *Seats*: a free seat is assigned; keep it. Choosing another one costs money.
  - *Services*: bags, priority and other extras are offered; continue without them.
  - *Confirm*: tick the conditions of carriage, then **Check in now**.
- **Passport**: not asked on a flight inside the EU (Schengen). Expect the passport step
  on a flight leaving the Schengen area; the owner types it.
- **Boarding pass**: shown on the confirmation page (download, or add to a phone wallet).
  That download is the owner's click.
- **Booked through an OTA**: an OTA's automatic check-in can miss a Eurowings leg when the
  passport details were never sent to the OTA. The airline's own check-in works with the
  carrier PNR from the OTA mail.
